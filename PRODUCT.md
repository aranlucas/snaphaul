# Snaphaul

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Resellers turning item photos into marketplace listing drafts. The repetitive
work is writing and checking another listing after sourcing and photographing it.

## Product Purpose

Clear the blank page while keeping the seller in control of the final claims.
See the [README](README.md) for setup, supported marketplaces and beta boundaries,
and the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) for proposed priorities and acceptance criteria.

## Product Principles

- Photos start the workflow; seller knowledge completes it and takes precedence over inference.
- Drafts stay editable and the seller stays responsible for condition, authenticity and marketplace eligibility.
- Make waits and recovery visible without inventing provider progress.
- State price and quota limitations plainly. Asking-price comparisons are not sold-price evidence.
- Label illustrative listings and test fixtures; there is no measured conversion or time-savings evidence.

Local QA uses synthetic photos and mocked providers, without live provider calls,
production data, credentials, paid services or infrastructure changes.

## Brand and Accessibility

Keep the Snaphaul name and camera mark. Follow the [design rationale](DESIGN.md#the-contact-sheet-studio)
when extending the interface. Support keyboard and touch use, small screens and
reduced motion.
