# Snaphaul

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Resellers turning item photos into listing drafts for eBay, Etsy, Poshmark and Mercari. The delegated brief confirms the photo-to-listing workflow and authorizes an ambitious frontend redesign.

## Product Purpose

Clear the blank page: upload one to five item photos, choose a marketplace, add seller knowledge, generate suggestions, review and copy them into the marketplace.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Free anonymous beta with ten successful generations per browser cookie session. This is an advisory allowance, not an enforceable per-person quota; cookies can reset and concurrent requests can share a count. The backend returns title, description, specifics, tags, category, photo observations and a suggested USD price range. Optional eBay comparisons are live asking prices, not sold-price evidence. Photos/details are sent to OpenRouter; the app does not persist them. It does not publish listings, save history or guarantee valuations. Keep PR20 input bounds and API contracts intact. JPEG, PNG, WebP and GIF are supported. Seller details are authoritative. Generated claims and marketplace eligibility require seller review.

## Brand Commitments

Snaphaul name and existing camera mark. Lucas authorized bold Impeccable frontend improvements and delegated composition selection. Preserve factual claims, existing visibility and deployment behavior. No production photos, provider calls, accounts, credentials, paid services or infrastructure in local QA.

## Evidence on Hand

README.md, lib/marketplaces.ts, lib/generate-listing.ts and app/api/generate/route.ts establish product truth. No testimonials or conversion data. Landing example will be an explicitly labeled hand-authored illustration; browser test responses are clearly labeled fixtures.

## Product Principles

- Photos start the workflow; seller knowledge completes it.
- Drafts stay editable and the seller stays responsible.
- Make waits and recovery visible without inventing provider progress.
- State price and quota limitations plainly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard and touch usability, reduced motion and small-screen support are explicit brief requirements.
