## r/Flipping post

**Title:** I built a free tool that turns item photos into ready-to-paste listings (eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Etsy) — 10 free generations, no signup

**Body:**

I source way faster than I list, and my bottleneck was never the photos — it was writing the 47th title of the day. So I built Snaphaul: snap 1–5 photos, pick your marketplace, and it writes the whole listing in one shot:

- Title suggestions tailored to the selected marketplace
- Full description (condition, measurements, flaws if you note them)
- Item specifics, tags, category suggestion
- A suggested price with reasoning

The same photos can be used for eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, or Etsy drafts. The prompts tailor titles, descriptions and tags to the selected marketplace. Review every claim, price suggestion and marketplace requirement before publishing; generated drafts can be wrong.

The beta offers 10 successful generations per browser cookie allowance, no signup. It can reset and is not a per-person quota. I'd love feedback from people who list more than I do — especially on the Poshmark and Mercari outputs, and whether the price ranges match your gut.
