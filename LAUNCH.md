# Snaphaul Launch Kit

## r/Flipping post

**Title:** I built a free tool that turns item photos into ready-to-paste listings (eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Etsy) — 10 free listings, no signup

**Body:**

I source way faster than I list, and my bottleneck was never the photos — it was writing the 47th title of the day. So I built Snaphaul: snap 1–5 photos, pick your marketplace, and it writes the whole listing in one shot:

- Title within the marketplace's character limit, keywords front-loaded
- Full description (condition, measurements, flaws if you note them)
- Item specifics, tags, category suggestion
- A suggested price with reasoning

Same photo session works for eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, and Etsy — each output is formatted for that platform's actual rules (80-char eBay keyword titles, 50-char Poshmark, hashtag descriptions, Etsy's 13 long-tail tags), because rewriting one listing four ways is the actual time sink.

It's free for 10 listings, no signup. Would genuinely love feedback from people who list more than I do — especially on the Poshmark and Mercari outputs, and whether the price ranges match your gut.
